Divorce & Family Law Attorney

I am a former BigLaw attorney and corporate executive who launched a contemporary boutique firm in 2016, designed to contribute to the local economy by helping small businesses leverage the law to make and keep more money. I am grateful to have served so many wonderful businesses.

In 2021, I sold the business practice. Right about that time, I found myself separated from my husband of 17 years, lawyered up, mired in the messy emotions and financial burdens of divorce, and parsing out the pieces of a life I built with my partner into piles of assets and liabilities. That experience changed how I view the legal process of divorce, which is broken (to say the least).

Today, Brava Law serves the needs of families mired in life's transitions. Our mission is to free good people from the trap of miserable relationships that no longer serve them. I'm a warrior for my clients when "friendly" isn't an option, ensuring their interests are vigorously protected. And for divorcing couples who want to protect their best interests, our One-Per-Divorce Alternatives are the key to a liberated future for your family.

YOLO. So create the life you want. Go have a great adventure with "your person." But if the person who was once "your person" has decided to paint a picture of a beautiful life without them and then make it real, YOLO means Get the Divorce. I'm here for the legal stuff.
