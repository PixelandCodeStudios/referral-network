Teresa Fitzpatrick

Civil Litigation & Estate Planning Attorney

Teresa Fitzpatrick is a respected civil litigator who founded Fitzpatrick & Fitzpatrick in 2004. With extensive experience as a staff attorney with the United Auto Workers Legal Services, she brings a strong foundation in advocating for clients' rights.

Specializing in wills and trusts, estate planning, and consumer litigation, Teresa provides comprehensive legal solutions for individuals and families throughout Florida. Her practice is built on the principle of protecting your rights and advocating for your future.

As an active member of the St. Petersburg Bar Association and the Women's Lawyers Group of Pinellas County, Teresa remains connected to the legal community and committed to serving her clients with dedication and expertise. Whether you need assistance with estate planning or civil litigation matters, Teresa brings the experience and care your case deserves.
